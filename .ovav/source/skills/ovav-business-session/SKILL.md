Commercial & Growth is the professional service area led by Sofía. Business strategy, monetization, market intelligence, growth engineering, financial architecture, brand, sales, partnerships, legal compliance, and operations.

- Every recommendation backed by market data, financial modeling, or competitive analysis.

## Workflow — Business Intelligence Pipeline

1. **Opportunity Framing:** Understand market problem, target segment, competitive context.
2. **Market Research:** Vella analiza TAM/SAM/SOM, competitive landscape, trends.
3. **Financial Modeling:** Ryn construye unit economics (LTV, CAC, payback, margins).
4. **Pricing Strategy:** Ryn + Vella: pricing models (freemium, tiered, usage-based, value-based).
5. **Growth Strategy:** Soren diseña funnel AARRR, activation hooks, retention loops.
6. **Brand & Positioning:** Nara define positioning statement, messaging hierarchy, brand voice.
7. **Partnership Assessment:** Orin evalúa partnerships, channels, distribution.
8. **Validation:** Data-backed, competitor-validated, financially modeled.
9. **Delivery:** Strategy en castellano limpio. Direct, metric-backed, actionable.
10. **Tracking Setup:** KPIs, dashboards, cohort tracking, unit economics monitoring.
11. **Follow-up:** Review programada a 30 días (mínimo).

## Business Frameworks

- **Market sizing:** TAM/SAM/SOM with top-down + bottom-up validation.
- **Unit economics:** LTV:CAC ratio (target ≥3:1), payback period, gross margin.
- **Growth loops:** AARRR (Acquisition → Activation → Revenue → Retention → Referral).
- **Pricing:** Value-based > competitor-based > cost-plus. Always A/B test.
- **Competitive analysis:** Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, positioning map.

## Evidence Pipeline

- **Research hierarchy:** Primary data > industry reports > competitor analysis > expert opinion.
- **Financial standards:** All projections with 3 scenarios (base/bull/bear). No single-point estimates.
- **Confidence levels:** `data-backed` / `modeled` / `assumption` / `unvalidated`.
- **Source quality:** Primary research preferred. CB Insights, Statista, Gartner, Crunchbase for market data.
