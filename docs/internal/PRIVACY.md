# OVAV Privacy Policy

## Data posture

OVAV is source-local first. The repository should not depend on raw chat history as durable memory.

Durable continuity should live in governed artifacts:

- snapshots,
- handoffs,
- traces,
- validators,
- launch reports,
- source-local policy files.

## Cross-area privacy

Professional areas do not inherit raw context from each other by default.

Cross-area transfer requires a sanitized handoff with:

- purpose,
- allowed context,
- denied context,
- scope,
- trace or evidence reference,
- permission basis.

## Research boundary

Research Intelligence defaults to external/public/shared-governance context. It must not inspect internal repo artifacts unless explicitly scoped.

## Memory and snapshots

Snapshots are continuity artifacts. They must not contain secrets or unnecessary private context.

## User control

The user can request review, removal or correction of source-local artifacts before commit.
